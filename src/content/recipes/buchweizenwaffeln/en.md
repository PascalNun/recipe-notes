ingredientSections:
  - title: For the waffle batter
    items:
      - 2–3 tsp chia seeds
      - 8–12 tsp water
      - 50 g coconut oil
      - 60 g sugar
      - 200 g buckwheat flour
      - 50 g cornstarch
      - 250 ml plant drink
      - 1/2 packet baking powder
      - 1/2 tsp cinnamon
      - 1 tsp vinegar
  - title: Also
    items:
      - a little neutral oil or coconut oil for greasing the waffle iron if needed
steps:
  - number: 1
    title: Soak the chia seeds
    text: Mix the chia seeds and water thoroughly in a small bowl. Leave for about 10 to 15 minutes, until the mixture becomes gel-like.
    note: Stir again after a few minutes so that no dry clumps remain. Use about 8 tsp water for 2 tsp chia seeds and about 12 tsp for 3 tsp.
    image: ./assets/step-01.webp
    imageAlt: A small ceramic bowl contains a lightly gelled chia-seed and water mixture with a small spoon resting inside
  - number: 2
    title: Mix the waffle batter
    text: Gently melt the coconut oil and leave it to cool briefly. Combine the buckwheat flour, cornstarch, sugar, baking powder and cinnamon in a large bowl. Add the plant drink, soaked chia seeds, liquid coconut oil and vinegar, then whisk to an even batter.
    note: Mix only as long as necessary and leave the batter to rest for about 5 minutes. It should be thick but easy to portion; if it becomes unusually firm, stir in a little additional plant drink.
    image: ./assets/step-02.webp
    imageAlt: A whisk stirs smooth, thick grey-brown buckwheat batter in a large pale bowl
  - number: 3
    title: Prepare the waffle iron
    text: Preheat a classic round heart-shaped waffle iron at a medium to slightly high setting, around three quarters of its maximum power. Lightly grease the iron if needed.
    note: With a well-coated surface and sufficient coconut oil in the batter, additional fat may not be necessary.
    image: ./assets/step-03.webp
    imageAlt: A small ladle pours buckwheat batter into the centre of an open round waffle iron with five shallow heart segments
  - number: 4
    title: Cook the waffles
    text: Spoon a suitable amount of batter into the centre of the lower plate, close the waffle iron and cook for about 4 to 6 minutes depending on the appliance, until evenly browned and easy to release. Repeat with the remaining batter.
    note: Remove each waffle carefully and initially cool them separately on a wire rack so that trapped steam does not soften them.
    image: ./assets/step-04.webp
    imageAlt: Several thin golden- to grey-brown five-segment heart waffles cool separately on a wire rack

tips:
  - Let the chia seeds gel fully before adding them to the batter.
  - Allow the melted coconut oil to cool briefly and avoid using the remaining ingredients straight from the refrigerator.
  - Grease the waffle iron only if needed and use a slightly restrained portion for the first waffle.
  - Initially cool finished waffles separately on a wire rack.
  - A toaster works better than a microwave for crisp reheated waffles.
  - For reliably gluten-free preparation, use labelled gluten-free buckwheat flour, starch and baking powder and a waffle iron uncontaminated by gluten-containing batters. Check plant drinks as well, particularly oat drink.
