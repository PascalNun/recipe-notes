ingredientSections:
  - title: For the yeast dough
    items:
      - 300 g German Type 405 wheat flour or plain flour
      - 150 g lukewarm water
      - 30 g agave syrup
      - 6 g rapeseed oil
      - 4 g dried yeast
      - 2 g salt
  - title: For the red bean paste
    items:
      - 50 g dried azuki beans
      - 30 g agave syrup
      - water for soaking and cooking
      - optional small pinch of salt
steps:
  - number: 1
    title: Prepare the azuki beans
    text: Rinse the azuki beans thoroughly and soak them in plenty of water, preferably overnight. Drain away the soaking water, add fresh water and cook the beans until soft, about 60 to 90 minutes depending on the variety.
    note: The beans are ready when they can be crushed easily between your fingers.
    image: ./assets/step-01.webp
    imageAlt: Red-brown azuki beans simmer in a small pale pot with its lid set to one side
  - number: 2
    title: Make the red bean paste
    text: Drain the beans, reserving a little of the cooking water. Purée the beans as finely as possible with the agave syrup and, optionally, a small pinch of salt using an immersion blender. Add a tablespoon of cooking water at a time if needed.
    note: The paste should be smooth, thick and easy to shape. Leave it to cool completely, then refrigerate until needed.
    image: ./assets/step-02.webp
    imageAlt: An immersion blender turns cooked azuki beans into a smooth thick paste in a tall ceramic vessel
  - number: 3
    title: Make the yeast dough
    text: Knead the flour, salt, dried yeast, agave syrup, lukewarm water and rapeseed oil into a smooth, elastic dough.
    note: Cover and leave to rise for about 60 to 90 minutes, until the dough has roughly doubled in volume.
    image: ./assets/step-03.webp
    imageAlt: A dark stand mixer kneads smooth pale yeast dough with a dough hook in a stainless steel bowl
  - number: 4
    title: Shape the buns
    text: Divide the dough into eight equal portions. Shape each piece into a ball, then roll or press it into a circle, leaving the centre slightly thicker than the edge. Place about one to two teaspoons of chilled bean paste in the centre. Bring the edges upward and seal them carefully. Set each finished bun seam-side down on a small square of baking paper.
    imageAlt: Four images show the dough circle, centred bean paste, sealed pleated crown and the turned bun on baking paper
    sequenceImages:
      first: ./assets/step-04-stage-1.webp
      second: ./assets/step-04-stage-2.webp
      third: ./assets/step-04-stage-3.webp
      fourth: ./assets/step-04-stage-4.webp
  - number: 5
    title: Steam
    text: Leave the shaped buns to rest for another 20 to 30 minutes. Steam them in a bamboo basket for about 15 minutes.
    note: At the end of the cooking time, leave the lid closed for about five more minutes before removing the buns.
    image: ./assets/step-05.webp
    imageAlt: Four soft buns on baking-paper squares steam in a bamboo basket above a wide dark wok

tips:
  - Let the bean paste cool completely.
  - A firm paste makes shaping considerably easier.
  - Keep the centre of the dough slightly thicker than its edge.
  - Do not open the lid immediately after steaming, so the buns do not collapse.
