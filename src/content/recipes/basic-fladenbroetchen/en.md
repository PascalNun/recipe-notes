ingredientSections:
  - title: For the yeast dough
    items:
      - 750 g wheat flour
      - 525 ml lukewarm water
      - 1 packet dried yeast
      - 10 g salt
      - spices as desired
  - title: Possible spices
    items:
      - turmeric
      - paprika
      - garlic powder
      - dried rosemary
      - thyme
      - Italian herbs
steps:
  - number: 1
    title: Make the dough
    text: Place the flour, dried yeast, salt and chosen spices in a large bowl. Add the lukewarm water and knead by hand or with a stand mixer and dough hook until smooth and elastic.
    note: The dough may remain soft and slightly sticky. Do not work in unnecessary extra flour. Cover and leave to rise for about 90 minutes, until it has roughly doubled in volume.
    image: ./assets/step-01.webp
    imageAlt: A dark stand mixer kneads soft yeast dough with a pale dough hook in a stainless-steel bowl
  - number: 2
    title: Shape the rounds
    text: Turn the risen dough onto a lightly floured work surface and divide it into ten roughly equal portions. Gently shape the portions into round, flat rolls and place them apart on a baking tray lined with baking paper.
    note: Handle the dough only as much as necessary so that it retains as much air as possible.
    image: ./assets/step-02.webp
    imageAlt: Soft risen yeast dough is divided into ten portions on a floured surface; several are already round and flat
  - number: 3
    title: Leave to rise again
    text: Cover the ten shaped rounds and leave them to rest for another 30 minutes, until visibly risen. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 200 °C fan.
    image: ./assets/step-03.webp
    imageAlt: Ten soft unbaked flatbread rolls sit apart on baking paper beneath a loosely lifted cloth
  - number: 4
    title: Bake
    text: Bake the flatbread rolls in the preheated oven for about 20 to 25 minutes, until lightly golden and cooked through. Remove them from the oven and leave to cool on a wire rack.
    image: ./assets/step-04.webp
    imageAlt: Several flat, gently irregular flatbread rolls cool on a wire rack with lightly golden surfaces

storage: Leave the rolls to cool completely. Stored airtight, they keep at room temperature for about two to three days. For longer storage, freeze them airtight in portions, thaw at room temperature and refresh for a few minutes in the oven.

tips:
  - Lightly flour your hands and work surface for shaping instead of working a large amount of extra flour into the dough.
  - The first rise is complete when the volume has roughly doubled.
  - Spices can be mixed directly into the flour.
  - Turmeric works well for mild seasoning and a warm yellow colour.
  - Let the rolls cool completely before freezing them.
  - Refresh briefly in the oven before serving.
