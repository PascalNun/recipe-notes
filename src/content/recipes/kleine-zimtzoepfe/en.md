ingredientSections:
  - title: For the yeast dough
    items:
      - 600 g flour
      - 250 ml oat milk
      - 60 g neutral oil
      - 30 g sugar
      - a little liquid stevia
      - 1 sachet dried yeast
      - 1 tsp salt
      - optional vanilla
      - optional cardamom
  - title: For the filling
    items:
      - about 2–3 tsp cinnamon, depending on the preferred intensity
      - 2–4 tbsp agave syrup
      - 1–2 tbsp sugar
      - optional splash of oat milk
      - optional small splash of oil
steps:
  - number: 1
    title: Make the yeast dough
    text: Warm the oat milk gently and stir in the sugar, stevia and dried yeast. Place the flour and salt in a large bowl, add the liquid and oil, then knead for several minutes to form a smooth dough.
    note: Cover and leave to rise, usually for about 60–90 minutes, until the dough has roughly doubled in volume.
    image: ./assets/step-01.webp
    imageAlt: Smooth risen yeast dough in a speckled ceramic bowl
  - number: 2
    title: Shape the braids
    text: Roll the risen dough into a rectangle and brush the surface evenly with agave syrup. Distribute the cinnamon and sugar evenly over it. Divide the prepared dough into 14 pieces of roughly equal size. Cut or divide each piece lengthways into three strands, leaving the strands connected at the top. Plait the three strands into a small braid, pinch the end gently and tuck it slightly underneath if needed. Place the braids on a baking tray.
    note: Leave to rise again, usually for about 15–20 minutes, until the braids look visibly puffed and more relaxed.
    imageAlt: Four images show how to fill, divide and plait one small three-strand braid
    sequenceImages:
      first: ./assets/step-02-stage-1.webp

      third: ./assets/step-02-stage-3-v2.webp
      fourth: ./assets/step-02-stage-4.webp
  - number: 3
    title: Bake
    text: Bake in a fan oven at 175–180 °C for about 15–20 minutes. The braids should be lightly golden and remain tender.
    image: ./assets/step-03.webp
    imageAlt: Six lightly golden elongated cinnamon braids on a dark baking tray
