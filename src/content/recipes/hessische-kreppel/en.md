ingredientSections:
  - title: For the yeast dough
    items:
      - 600 g flour
      - 125 g margarine
      - 70 g sugar
      - 1 sachet dried yeast or 42 g fresh yeast
      - 250 ml oat milk, lukewarm
      - 1 pinch salt
  - title: Also needed
    items:
      - oil for deep-frying
steps:
  - number: 1
    title: Activate the yeast
    text: Stir the yeast into the lukewarm oat milk with one teaspoon of the sugar.
    note: Leave to stand for about 10 minutes.
    image: ./assets/step-01.webp
    imageAlt: Activated yeast with fine bubbles in lukewarm oat milk
  - number: 2
    title: Make the dough
    text: Knead all the ingredients into a smooth yeast dough. Cover and leave to rise until it has roughly doubled in volume.
    note: The first rise usually takes about 60–90 minutes. The visible state of the dough matters more than an exact time.
    image: ./assets/step-02.webp
    imageAlt: Smooth risen yeast dough in a speckled ceramic bowl
  - number: 3
    title: Shape the Kreppel
    text: Roll the dough to a thickness of about 2 cm and cut out rounds.
    image: ./assets/step-03.webp
    imageAlt: Yeast dough rolled about two centimetres thick with cut rounds, a cutter and rolling pin
  - number: 4
    title: Fry
    text: Heat the oil to about 170–180 °C. Fry the Kreppel for 2–3 minutes on each side until golden, then drain them on kitchen paper.
    image: ./assets/step-04.webp
    imageAlt: Three Kreppel frying in a dark pot while small bubbles rise around a wooden skewer
