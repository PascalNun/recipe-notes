# Nutrition Estimate Notes

Nutrition values are optional editorial estimates. They are not laboratory analyses and must not be presented as exact when ingredient brands, finished weights, or frying absorption are unknown.

The public disclosure includes energy, fat, saturates, carbohydrate, sugars, fibre, protein, and salt for the complete recipe. A per-piece column is added only when the recipe defines a usable yield.

## Shared Method

- Reference composition values come from [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/), primarily Foundation and SR Legacy foods.
- Grams are used as the calculation unit. The 250 ml oat-milk quantities are approximated as 250 g.
- The midpoint is used for every stated ingredient range.
- Optional ingredients are excluded unless the estimate explicitly says otherwise.
- Energy in kilojoules is the calculated kcal value multiplied by 4.184.
- Salt combines recipe salt with sodium reported for the ingredients, using `salt = sodium × 2.5`.
- Display values are rounded for readability; calculations retain greater precision.

## Hessian Kreppel

Frying makes the result inherently less certain than a baked recipe. The displayed midpoint uses a representative yield of **18 Kreppel** and **7.5 g absorbed canola oil per piece**, or 135 g for the batch.

### Calculation basis

| Ingredient | Amount used |
| --- | ---: |
| all-purpose flour | 600 g |
| 80% fat salted margarine | 125 g |
| granulated sugar | 70 g |
| oat milk | 250 g |
| active dry yeast | 7 g |
| absorbed canola oil | 135 g |
| added salt | 0.4 g |

| --- | ---: | ---: |
| energy | 19,580 kJ / 4,680 kcal | 1,090 kJ / 260 kcal |
| fat | 248.1 g | 13.8 g |
| saturates | 30.4 g | 1.7 g |

The actual result changes with the 15–20 piece yield, margarine brand, frying temperature, proofing, surface area, draining, and oil absorption. A measured revision should weigh the frying oil before and after cooking and account for oil remaining in the vessel and on the draining paper.
