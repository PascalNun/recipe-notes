# Nutrition Estimate Notes

Nutrition is optional editorial information, not a recipe-completion requirement. It must never be inferred as an exact value when ingredient brands, finished weights, or cooking absorption are unknown.

## Hessian Kreppel

The public-facing estimate is approximately **220–320 kcal per piece** for a batch yielding 15–20 Kreppel.

### Reproducible baseline

Reference energy values are from USDA FoodData Central and are expressed per 100 g unless noted otherwise.

| Ingredient | Recipe amount | Reference energy | Batch energy |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| all-purpose wheat flour | 600 g | 362 kcal | 2,172 kcal |
| 80% fat margarine | 125 g | 717 kcal | 896 kcal |
| granulated sugar | 70 g | 387 kcal | 271 kcal |
| oat milk | 250 ml, approximated as 250 g | 45 kcal | 113 kcal |
| active dry yeast | 7 g | 325 kcal | 23 kcal |

Baseline batch energy before absorbed frying oil: approximately **3,474 kcal**.

This equals approximately **174–232 kcal per piece** before absorbed oil, depending on whether the batch yields 20 or 15 pieces.

### Frying-oil assumption

The recipe does not define an absorbed oil weight, and oil absorption varies with dough temperature, proofing, frying temperature, time, surface area, and draining. The editorial estimate therefore uses a clearly labelled working range of **5–10 g absorbed neutral oil per piece**.

Using 884 kcal per 100 g vegetable oil, this adds approximately **44–88 kcal per piece**. Combining the lowest-yield-independent baseline with the oil range gives approximately **218–320 kcal per piece**, rounded editorially to **220–320 kcal**.

This is intentionally a broad estimate. A future measured version should weigh the frying oil before and after cooking and account for oil remaining in the vessel and on the draining paper.
